## rKS — Attention & KOL/Mindshare Layer

Attention and KOL/Mindshare scoring. Tracks influence signals, KOL activity, and mindshare dynamics across crypto communities.

## rPS — Trading Setup & Technical Scoring Layer

Trading setup and technical scoring. Identifies actionable trading setups with structured technical analysis signals.

## rFS — Extended Flows Layer

Extended flows analysis. Tracks capital flow patterns, fund movements, and cross-protocol flow dynamics.

## rCG — Cluster Graph & Similarity Layer

Cluster graph and similarity analysis. Maps relationships between tokens, protocols, and wallets to identify hidden connections and similarity clusters.

## rCC — Coordinated Capital Detection Layer

Coordinated capital detection. Identifies patterns of coordinated buying, selling, and wallet activity that may indicate manipulation or organized capital movements.

---

## Access

Pilot modules are not available as standalone APIs. To access pilot modules:

- **Enterprise customers** (ENT-AGENTIC, ENT-INTEL, ENT-WHITE) can request pilot module integration as part of their engagement.
- **Data Factory customers** (ENT-FABRIC) can include pilot module outputs in custom dataset builds.
